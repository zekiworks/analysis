How the styles are found

Models. The Logic leaderboard's decision rows (one row per model by the site's rule) whose setup has complete English standard runs on Logic (1,125 questions) and Math (2,163) and a complete “I don't know” run on both: 38 models. Rows with web or on a sample are left out, as is any row missing a run (never filled in). Left out: Perplexity Decider 27B v1.2 (API · with web) (with web).

Numbers. Logic and Math accuracy: the standard runs, as on this site. “I don't know” rate: the answers that chose “I don't know” (option F) over all answers of the model's two “I don't know” runs, Logic and Math pooled (3,288 answers); not the non-hallucination rate F / (F + wrong). Each number is turned into a z-score over these models (mean 0, SD 1).

Clustering. k-means (best of 100 seeded restarts, seed 4317) and Ward clustering on the three z-scores for k = 2–6. Agreement: the adjusted Rand index (ARI) of the two (1 = the same groups; label-free). Stability: 500 bootstrap replicates (seed 4317) of each resampling unit, the questions (one resampled question set per benchmark, the same for every model and for both its runs) and the models (drawn with replacement; each model drawn is compared once). In every replicate the three numbers, the z-scores and both clusterings are refitted (k-means: 20 restarts) and compared with the full-data result by ARI on the models present. Same partners: per model, the mean share of its style partners it keeps in a replicate (Jaccard of the two partner sets, itself included), k-means at that k.

Choosing k. The rule for k is chosen by us (analyst-selected): among the k at which k-means and Ward agree (ARI ≥ 0.80), the one with the highest mean stability (the mean of the four bootstrap stabilities: k-means and Ward, questions and models resampled); a tie to the smaller k. If no k agrees, the k with the highest min(agreement, mean stability). Chosen: k = 6.

Names. A style's name from its mean z-scores (Logic, Math, IDK): skill = the mean of the two accuracy z-scores, Strong at or above +0.5, Low-scoring at or below −0.5, else Middle; IDK at or above +0.5 'cautious', at or below −0.5 'decisive'. Middle with an IDK word is that word alone ('Cautious', 'Decisive'). Styles are listed by mean skill, highest first.
