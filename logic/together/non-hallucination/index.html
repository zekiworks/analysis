Logic and Math · Non-hallucination

Non-hallucination on Logic and Math

This test is not a third question set: it pools the 1,125 Logic questions and the 2,163 Math questions into one set of 3,288 questions and scores every model on all of them together. Only models that have finished both Logic and Math are included; the others are listed below as not in Logic and Math yet. Five options each (A–E). Decision-model setups score every option and pick one; chat-model setups write a reply with an answer letter. Method.

Provisional: decision-model runs are still in progress; 22 of 25 planned decision setups have finished Math so far.

Five options, the standard run: how many answers a setup's own probability lets it keep at ≥99% and how many of those were wrong, and how well the probability separates right from wrong answers (AUROC).

(b) Explicit “I don't know” option

A sixth option F on the same questions (mode dont_know runs): non-hallucination = F / (F + wrong), with the attempt rate (right + wrong) / N and the accuracy when answering right / (right + wrong). A setup that works with probabilities may rarely choose F; read it on (a) and on the comparison with its own confidence.
