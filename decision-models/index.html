When should you trust a decision model?

Our reading comprehension and grammar test: 2,198 questions answered by 26 decision models, with chat models for comparison. A decision model scores each answer option and picks one. Grammar questions test whether a model knows a rule and can apply it. Every answer is public.

  • 2,198 questions
  • 20 topics
  • 26 decision models
  • 51 setups

Results 82d0982b, updated 22 October 2026; answer key version 2.

The questions are in native Turkish (not translated).

Among decision models, none led on every measure:

  • Accuracy: Liquid d1-ultra, clearly ahead at 77.1%; then GLiDE v1.2, GLiDE and Deciders v1.1 and v1.2, at 66.9% to 68.5%.
  • Calibration gap: Luna (Decisions API) had the smallest gap among decision models, without refit. With a refit fitted on other passages of this question set, the measured gap fell for many models. A refit does not change accuracy, and other tasks may give different results.
  • Estimated saving: d1-ultra, the biggest in all 10 LLM setups.
  • Reading vs grammar: decision models got up to 92.1% of the reading questions right, but at most 70.7% of the grammar questions.
