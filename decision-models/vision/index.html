More about this page

Info What we found, the measure and how the benchmark was run

About this test

Accuracy: the share of the 763 questions answered right (a refusal or an answer outside the options counts as wrong); in the tables its 95% interval shows when you hover over or tap a number.

Download: vision.json (every number on this page, and each answer's pick and option probabilities; no question text, page image or OCR text).

What we found

All 12 image models were less accurate with the picture alone (B) than with the text (A): by 5.9 points (d1-2B) to 29.2 points (KnowLine-31B-Gen1.5), each difference significant in the exact McNemar test.

With the picture added to the text (C), 8 of the 12 were more accurate than with the text alone, by 0.8 to 4.8 points; the gains of Jev 1.14, OneJev-27B and SubQ Snap 1.1 (4.3 to 4.8 points) are significant, the others are not. d1-ultra, d1-2B, GLiDE 27B and KnowLine-31B-Gen1.5 were less accurate with the picture (1.4, 2.4, 0.3 and 3.0 points), significantly for KnowLine-31B-Gen1.5.

Through an OCR model (D), 14 of the 15 models with an OCR route were less accurate than with the text: their best route was 2.2 to 7.9 points below condition A. GLiDE 2B's best route was 0.7 points above its text, without a paired test to tell that from chance. For the 9 image models with both, every OCR route was more accurate than the picture alone (B): the best route by 3.1 to 25.7 points, significant for 8 of the 9 (not for Jev 1.14).

The most accurate: with the text, KnowLine-31B-Gen1.5 (79.0%); with the picture alone, d1-ultra (50.1%); with both, KnowLine-31B-Gen1.5 (76.0%); through an OCR model, KnowLine-31B-Gen1.5 (75.5%).

Each cell is one pass over the 763 questions. Each test compares two conditions of the same model on the same questions, one comparison at a time, without an adjustment for the number of comparisons; the OCR routes have no paired test against the text (A).

How the benchmark was run

Conditions

  • A: clean text: the published text-track run (the same questions, prompts and options), graded against the bank as it stands now
  • B: the crop(s), passage first, as the whole state; instructions 'Which option is correct?'; the question's own option letters as the choices, each described by its letter only
  • C: condition A's text and options, with the crop(s) after the text; A's instructions ('Which option is the correct answer to this Turkish multiple-choice question?')
  • D: one OCR model's output for the crop(s) (passage first, joined by a blank line) as the state, as its official export gives it, with layout tags removed (Unlimited-OCR-2's block boxes and page marks) and markup kept; instructions 'Which option is correct?'; option letters only, as B

Each cell is one run. It is one pass over the 763 questions, not an average of repeated runs (6 passes were resumed after an interruption; see Models and cost under Run details).

Run details: pictures and leak checks, OCR models, models and cost

Pictures of the questions

The bank's per-question boxes (questions.bbox_json, passages.bbox_json, from its extraction run), on pages rendered from the source PDF with PyMuPDF at zoom 2.5 (180 dpi, 1600 x 1990 px), padded 4 px; on pages that print an answer key, each crop stops 6 px above the top line of the key box; crops under 280 px high are padded with white below, and crops over 1568 px on a side are scaled down to 1568 (TypeSafe's image limits; every model gets the same files). 767 images for 763 questions; 129 crops stop above an answer key box, 3 were padded and 1 scaled. Questions per topic: Word and phrase meanings: 73; Sentence meanings: 86; How a writer explains and develops ideas: 3; Topic and main point of a paragraph: 2; How a paragraph fits together: 54; Details that support a paragraph’s main point: 1; Word types, such as nouns and verbs: 47; Groups of words built around a noun: 19; Verbs and their forms: 18; Verb forms used like nouns or modifiers: 37; Word endings and what they do: 53; How words are built: 31; The roles of words and phrases in a sentence: 46; How verbs show who acts and who receives the action: 20; Types of sentences: 23; Questions combining several grammar topics: 94; Sound patterns and changes: 23; Spelling: 68; Punctuation: 32; Problems in sentence wording: 33.

We sent every crop at its own size: at most 1568 pixels a side (one crop is 1568; the rest are at most 1169). The input tokens each API reported match its documented billing grid at those sent sizes, on all 763 questions: TypeSafe's Jev, 2 tokens per 28 by 28 pixel cell plus a fixed 354 to 361 tokens (375 to 380 with two crops); Liquid's d1-ultra, 44 plus 1.5 tokens per 32 by 32 pixel patch; OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna through the Decisions API, 130 plus 1 token per 32 by 32 pixel patch with the default detail setting. A downscale before counting, to 1024 pixels or less, would have changed these counts. This shows the size at which each API counted and billed an image, not what the model saw inside it. TypeSafe documents that it rejects images outside 280 to 1568 pixels a side instead of resizing them; that is a rule about accepted inputs, not a guarantee about every internal tensor. Liquid's and OpenAI's image dimensions after tokenization are not observable to us.

Checked by eye for leaks: 20 crops, with 0 leaks found. The first detector looked only for pink or dark lines and missed the blue key boxes of some units, whose keys are printed in plain sight (page 60 shows '1-C 2-C ...'); it was replaced by a colour-blind test (any coloured or dark row across 85% of the width) before any model saw a crop; all 368 crops on key pages now stop 6 px above the box's top line (1796 or 1797 px), and no rendered page without a recorded key has such a box.

We also searched every OCR output, 3,068 in all (4 OCR models × 767 crops), for answer-key text: number-letter pairs as a key prints them (“1‑C 2‑C”) and key wording. None had key text. Six Baidu Unlimited-OCR-2 outputs each had one number-letter pair; in each, the number is the question’s own number and the letter is not its answer.

OCR models

  • Chandra OCR 2 (datalab-to/chandra-ocr-2 at af93b47): chandra-ocr 0.2.0 official vLLM path (chandra.model.InferenceManager(method='vllm'), the code behind the `chandra <input> <out> --method vllm` CLI) against a vLLM 0.32.1rc1.dev613+g8977b13a1 OpenAI-compatible server (local nightly venv on 1 GPU, started with the same arguments as chandra's `chandra_vllm` launcher: --dtype bfloat16 --max-model-len 18000 --max-num-seqs 64 --max-num-batched-tokens 8192 --enable-prefix-caching --mm-processor-kwargs {min_pixels: 3136, max_pixels: 6291456}; the launcher itself would run the vllm/vllm-openai:v0.17.0 docker image, which was not used). Each crop was loaded with chandra.input.load_image (upscaled so the shorter side is >= 1536 px), resized by chandra's scale_to_fit, sent as PNG with the default 'ocr_layout' prompt (PROMPT_MAPPING['ocr_layout']); all 767 requests in one generate() call with 64 client threads. Decoding: temperature 0, top_p 0.1, max_tokens 12384 (chandra defaults); chandra's built-in retry re-generates an output with a detected repeat-token loop at temperature min(0.2*(n+1), 0.8), top_p 0.95 (up to 6 retries). The model emits layout HTML (<div data-bbox data-label> blocks); the stored text is chandra's official markdown export (BatchOutputItem.markdown = chandra.output.parse_markdown with the CLI defaults include_images=True, include_headers_footers=False, i.e. Page-Header/Page-Footer blocks dropped). dtype bfloat16. In this run chandra's client logged 39 repeat-token retry events (the retried pages' final text therefore comes from a sampled, non-greedy re-generation; chandra does not report which pages).
  • dots.mocr (dots-studio/dots.mocr at e539fbb): dots_mocr parser (github.com/rednote-hilab/dots.mocr @ 23f3e5612fb8, installed editable) official vLLM path: DotsMOCRParser.parse_file(<png>, prompt_mode='prompt_layout_all_en', fitz_preprocess=True), i.e. `python3 dots_mocr/parser.py <png>` with the CLI defaults (prompt_layout_all_en, top_p 1.0, max_completion_tokens 16384, dpi 200 fitz re-render of the image, no min/max_pixels override) except temperature 0.0 instead of the CLI's 0.1 for greedy decoding; requests go to a vLLM 0.32.1rc1.dev613+g8977b13a1 OpenAI-compatible server (local nightly venv, native DotsOCRForCausalLM implementation, launched as in the model card: --gpu-memory-utilization 0.9 --chat-template-content-format string --served-model-name model --trust-remote-code; model dtype bfloat16 from config). 64 images in flight. The parser's request function was replaced by an identical copy that also records finish_reason (to detect token-limit hits). The model returns layout JSON (bbox, category, text); the stored text is the parser's own markdown export (<name>.md = format_transformer.layoutjson2md(..., no_page_hf=False): cells in reading order joined by blank lines, Formula as LaTeX, Table as HTML, Picture cells as an inline base64 PNG image link); when the JSON fails to parse, the parser's OutputCleaner fallback text is stored (noted in failures).
  • Surya OCR 2 (datalab-to/surya-ocr-2 at 3b3d4cd): surya-ocr 0.22.1 official full-page OCR path (the code behind `surya_ocr <folder>`: surya.input.load.load_from_folder, then RecognitionPredictor(SuryaInferenceManager())(images, full_page=True)) against a vLLM 0.20.1 OpenAI-compatible server (vllm==0.20.1 from PyPI in an isolated venv on 1 GPU, the version of surya's vllm/vllm-openai:v0.20.1 docker image, started with the same arguments surya's VllmBackend gives that image for VLLM_GPU_TYPE=h100: --dtype bfloat16 --max-model-len 18000 --max-num-seqs 104 --max-num-batched-tokens 16384 --enable-prefix-caching --mm-processor-kwargs {min_pixels: 3136, max_pixels: 6291456} --speculative-config {method: mtp, num_speculative_tokens: 2}; docker itself was not used). The server venv pins transformers 5.17.0 (surya requires >=5.12.1): transformers >=5.18 loads this checkpoint's char-level tokenizer as the byte-level Qwen3_5Tokenizer, which corrupted every prompt and turned non-ASCII output letters (ı ğ ç ö ü ...) into U+FFFD/control bytes in a first run with vLLM nightly + transformers 5.20 (discarded). Attached via SURYA_INFERENCE_BACKEND=vllm, SURYA_INFERENCE_URL; client concurrency 96 (surya default for that sizing). Each crop is sent once as PNG (after surya's scale_to_fit) with the default full-page prompt HIGH_ACCURACY_BBOX_PROMPT; decoding: temperature 0, top_p 0.1, max_tokens 12288 (surya defaults; SURYA_FULLPAGE_REGEN off), surya's client retries a request that ends in a repeat-token loop at temperature min(0.2*(n+1), 0.8), top_p 0.95 (max 3 retries), and a page whose full-page HTML fails to parse or loops falls back to surya's layout + per-block OCR. The model emits HTML layout blocks (<div data-bbox data-label>); surya parses them into PageOCRResult.blocks in reading order with per-block inner HTML. Surya has no markdown/text export of its own (that lives in marker), so the stored text is the non-skipped blocks' `html` (surya's official per-block output, math in <math>, tables as <table>) joined in reading order with blank lines; bounding boxes and labels are dropped. dtype bfloat16. In this run: 101 client-side retry requests (non-greedy), 101 of them full-page; 2 pages used the layout + per-block fallback (12 layout/block requests).
  • Baidu Unlimited-OCR-2 (baidu/Unlimited-OCR-2 at 04647c7): Model card's Transformers path (trust_remote_code; the snapshot's modeling_unlimitedocr.py), README 'Single-page inference': AutoModel.from_pretrained(..., trust_remote_code=True, use_safetensors=True, torch_dtype=torch.bfloat16).eval().cuda(), then model.infer(tokenizer, image_file=<png>, output_path=<dir>, save_results=True) per crop with all decoding defaults: prompt built automatically as '<PAGE><image>Multi page parsing.' for one page, crop_mode=None -> gundam dynamic tiling for a single page (base_size 1024, image_size 640, max_crops 16), greedy argmax (temperature 0), PageCountGuard on with max_page_tokens=20000, max_length 131072, no n-gram blocking, DASH sparse attention (dash_topm 16). eval_mode=True only disables the console token streamer; return_dict=True only returns token ids/finish reason. Batch size 1 per model copy (the path has no batching); 8 worker processes, each with its own model copy, shared GPU 2 and took the crops round-robin; seconds = first worker start (after all models loaded) to last worker end. The model emits markdown with grounding refs (<|ref|>..<|/ref|><|det|>[[boxes]]<|/det|>) and a <PAGE> marker; the stored text is the model's own save_results output result.md (its _uocr_save_results: non-image refs/boxes removed, image refs replaced by ![](images/page_0_<i>.jpg) links, '<PAGE>' line kept). torch 2.13.0+cu130, transformers 4.52.1, dtype bfloat16.
