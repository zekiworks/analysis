Compared with Artificial Analysis

Artificial Analysis's Decision Index models, on our benchmark

Artificial Analysis's post of 22 October 2026 ranks 14 decision models on its Decision Index v1.0, a weighted average of four of its own evaluations (AA-Omniscience-Decision, HLE-MultiChoice, When2Call and WildGuardTest). 13 of them are on our leaderboard; Clef-Omni is not. This page gathers our numbers for those 13, from our published results: the numbers of our four charts in reply to that post come from here. Our tests are different from Artificial Analysis's, so the two rank different things. This page ranks nothing new and changes no number on the other pages.
