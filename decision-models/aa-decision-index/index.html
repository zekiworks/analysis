Artificial Analysis's post of 22 October 2026 ranks 14 decision models on its Decision Index v1.0, a weighted average of four of its own evaluations (AA-Omniscience-Decision, HLE-MultiChoice, When2Call and WildGuardTest). 13 of them are on our leaderboard; Clef-Omni is not. This page gathers our numbers for those 13, from our published results, and opens with our reply charts. It ranks nothing new and changes no number on the other pages.
