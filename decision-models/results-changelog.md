# Results changelog

What changed in each results version that went live (`results_version` in `results.json`), newest first. It starts with results 0eb1e828, the release that added Liquid d1-2B; earlier versions are in the repository's history.

## Results 5c638c7d (2 November 2026)

- New tab, “English reading” (`english-reading/`): the Text tab's 656 reading questions (units 1 to 6; the grammar questions left out) translated into English by Claude Opus 5.5 (effort high, through the Claude Code CLI), which in a separate request flagged the questions whose answer depends on Turkish wording. The 109 flagged questions are left out and 547 kept; no person checked the translations. Eight decision models answered the kept questions in English as on the Text tab (one question per request, each model's own settings), with the instructions naming English instead of Turkish and the options under “Options:” instead of “Seçenekler:”, in 28 runs: five each for Liquid d1-ultra, Liquid d1, Jev 1.14.0, Microsoft-Decision-1 and Mercury Decide, whose repeated Turkish runs changed answers, and one each for SubQ Snap 1.1, Drex Max 1.1 and GPT-6 Luna. Each model's first English run against its Text tab run on the same 547 questions (exact McNemar test; Holm's correction over the eight): Microsoft-Decision-1 scored higher in English (85.7% against 76.4%, adjusted p < 0.0001) and Liquid d1-ultra higher in Turkish (93.6% against 90.7%, adjusted p = 0.049); for the other six the difference could be chance. Liquid d1 was asked as `d1`, because Liquid's API no longer serves the `d1:free` name its Turkish runs used. Not compared: the hosted Fastino GLiDE, because since 10 October Fastino's API has answered with its glide_v2 engine, not the glide engine of its Turkish run. Not run: Perplexity Decider 27B v1.1 and Celeris-1 Decision. Not run by decision: the nine models that run on our GPUs. The tab sits apart from the leaderboard and changes none of its numbers.
- New and changed files: `english-reading.json`, the track's public record (the translator, both prompts, each reading question's flag reasons, whether it is kept and the SHA-256 of its English text; no question text, and the translations themselves are not published); `results.json` gains `english_reading`; `answers.json` gains `english_runs` and `english_prompts`, and every other run an `english` field (null); `runner/` gains the runner's `--english` mode, `run_english.sh` and `translate_reading.py`. No other number changed.

## Results 973d93d3 (2 November 2026)

- Hallucination tab: each model's run with “Bilmiyorum” against its five-option run held back by its own confidence on as many questions, as wrong answers on the same questions (exact McNemar test; Holm's correction over the 7 models that chose “Bilmiyorum” on at least 1% of the questions), in `results.json` (`dont_know.models[].vs_confidence`). Confidence left significantly fewer wrong answers for Liquid d1-ultra and Mammoth-1 (adjusted p = 0.016 each); for the other 5 the difference could be chance. The tab says so at the top and under What we found. SubQ Snap Preview stays not run, now by decision. The charts on the Text tab show the 20 most accurate decision models by default, instead of 8. No other number changed.

## Results 2c430f54 (1 November 2026)

- Added SubQ Snap 1.1 (`subq-snap-1.1`, public preview) through SubQ's Decisions API. Text: five runs over the 2,198 scored questions (runs 346, 387, 388, 389 and 390), 63.9% right, 12th of 37 decision models, the five runs identical. Page images: condition A from run 346 (58.1%), B (45.7%), C (62.9%) and D with each of the four OCR models (49.7% to 54.3%), over the 763 page-image questions (runs 62 to 67). Its probabilities arrive as 32-bit floats. As an experimental rule of ours for this model only, the check before its runs accepted sums within 32-bit rounding. Run 346 was paused twice while that rule's approval was confirmed; its note gives the effect on its request times. The SubQ Snap Preview rows are unchanged.
- "I don't know option" tab: SubQ Snap 1.1 added (run 392).
- From the new row: the summary's "Better than a flat guess" line now names SubQ Snap 1.1 with Perplexity Decider 27B v1.1 (31.5% each) after Open-Jev 27B v1.1. SubQ Snap Preview's places on the Routing tab move down one in front of Opus 5.5 low (seventeenth) and Astra low (fifteenth). One neighbour pair is replaced by two, and 16 comparisons have new Holm-adjusted p-values, because the correction now covers 2,628 pairs of runs instead of 2,556. Comparison letters gain the new row's letter where it joins a group; no tie between existing rows changed. The short chart labels now read "Snap Preview" and "Snap 1.1". No other existing number changed.

## Results 74c13f91 (1 November 2026)

- Added Base Labs' Elephant-1 and Mammoth-1 (open weights): `baselabs/elephant-1` at `b3f8a93` and `baselabs/mammoth-1` at `61cec44` on our GPU, through their model cards' loader (qev's DecisionEngine with the checkpoints' serve.patch). Text: five runs each over the 2,198 scored questions, each model's five runs identical. Mammoth-1 (runs 374, 377, 380, 383, 384): 67.1% right, 8th of 36 decision models. Elephant-1 (runs 373, 375, 376, 378, 379): 52.4% right, 26th of 36. Their loader rounds probabilities to 6 decimals, and the check before their runs allowed for it. Their cost is at Baseten's list prices for the same models. They are not on the Page images tab: their runtime reads images only through an engine without the attention the models were trained with.
- New tab, "I don't know option" (`dont-know/`): the Text tab's questions with a sixth option, "Bilmiyorum" (I don't know), always last and never correct. It covers ten decision models in 18 runs, with accuracy, attempt rate, non-hallucination rate and the mean probability of "Bilmiyorum". It sits apart from the leaderboard and changes none of its numbers.
- From the two new rows: the summary's accuracy line now names Mammoth-1 after Drex Max v1 and 1.1. SubQ Snap's places on the Routing tab move down one (tenth in front of Sonnet 5.5 high, sixteenth in front of Opus 5.5 low, fourteenth in front of Astra low). The paired tests between ranking neighbours pair each new row with the rows on either side of it, in place of two earlier neighbour pairs. 17 other comparisons have new Holm-adjusted p-values, because the correction now covers 2,556 pairs of runs instead of 2,415. No other existing number changed.
