# Results changelog

What changed in each results version that went live (`results_version` in `results.json`), newest first. It starts with results 0eb1e828, the release that added Liquid d1-2B; earlier versions are in the repository's history.

## Results 5fbf4cb3 (3 November 2026)

- Hallucination tab, the same number of answers: at Ümit's request, the Turkish comparison now covers every setup of the Text tab (73, decision models and chat and general models alike; hosted GLiDE through its five-option run 517), with Holm's correction over the 72 besides Liquid d1-ultra; English is unchanged (14 models, Holm over 13). Each model's p is as before, the resamples being the same; the adjusted p of the 10 models published in 64cea0e4 rises from 0.002 to 0.014, still significant. All 36 other decision models keep significantly more wrong answers than Liquid d1-ultra's 167 of 1,582; of the 36 chat and general models, 19 keep significantly fewer (from GPT-6 Astra's 4.4 expected wrong to Claude Sonnet 5.5's 114.3), 15 significantly more, and for 2 (Kimi K3.1 Pro and GLM-5.3-Flash) the difference could be chance. The plot keeps the 11 models with runs with the option. No other number changed.

## Results 64cea0e4 (3 November 2026)

- Hallucination tab, new section: the same number of answers, in `results.json` (`dont_know_same_answers`), from runs already published. Each model's five-option run keeps its k most confident answers, k being Liquid d1-ultra's answered count with “I don't know” (1,582 of the 2,198 Turkish questions, run 511; 496 of the 547 kept English questions, run 449), and tied scores at the k-th answer count in proportion. In Turkish, Liquid d1-ultra's five-option run keeps 167 wrong of 1,582 (10.6%), and all 10 other models keep significantly more, 7.7 to 26.5 points more (paired bootstrap over questions with 10,000 resamples, Holm's correction over the 10; hosted GLiDE with its five-option run 517). In English, it keeps 29 wrong of 496 (5.8%); 6 of the 13 others keep significantly more (Inception Mercury Decide, Microsoft-Decision-1, Elephant-1, Celeris-1 Decision, GPT-6 Luna and Perplexity Decider Flash v1.2) and none significantly fewer. Each model's curve of wrong of answers against answered is drawn with its runs with the option marked.
- Every “I don't know” table (the Hallucination tab's, the option word's, the English questions' on both tabs and the four models') now leads with Answered ((right + wrong) / questions; higher is better) and Wrong of answers (wrong / (right + wrong); lower is better), worked out from the published counts; the non-hallucination rate follows with its formula. No other number changed.

## Results ce52c83e (3 November 2026)

Analyses of the existing runs only, approved by Ümit; no new run, and no published measurement changed.

- Hallucination tab: each model's run with the option written “I don't know” (the 2,198 Turkish questions) against its five-option run held back by its own confidence at the same attempt rate, with the tab's derived method (the cut-off nearest the option run's attempt rate, the higher one on a tie), in `results.json` (`dont_know_option_word.vs_confidence`). Hosted GLiDE is compared with its new five-option run 517, not run 129. Each cut-off reached the option run's answered count exactly. Confidence left fewer wrong answers for all 7 models that chose the option on at least 1% of the questions (9 to 49 fewer); with the exact McNemar test of wrong answers and Holm's correction over those 7, only Liquid d1-ultra's difference is significant (216 wrong with “I don't know”, 167 by confidence; adjusted p 0.00046). Mammoth-1's, significant with “Bilmiyorum”, is not with “I don't know” (adjusted p 0.078). The banner and What we found now give both versions.
- Turkish vs English tab, new section: risk and coverage on the 547 kept questions, in `results.json` (`english_reading.coverage`): for each of the 14 compared models, the native operating points with Turkish “Bilmiyorum”, the Turkish wording control with “I don't know” and English “I don't know” (coverage, error rate among answered, right, wrong and abstained; Not measured where a model has no such run), and a confidence curve per language from its five-option runs, plotted per language. Full-bank runs are counted on the 547 questions only.
- The footer's “updated” date is now the day the results went out, 3 November 2026 (it was the leaderboard's newest run, 1 November). Here that is run 548's finish time; from the next release that changes the results, it is the export time, kept by any re-export that changes nothing.
