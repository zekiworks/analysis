PWS-Decisions: the decision models, with and without web

What this measures. Parallel Web Systems made PWS-Decisions. This is an end-to-end comparison of API setups with and without web search on its 100 public questions, which Parallel chose “for their difficulty and alignment with customer outcomes” (its methodology), around its customers' needs for current facts from the web. It is not a general ranking of models: without search, most setups score near random guessing, because the answers depend on dated web facts they were not given.

Each bar is the mean of our 3 runs per setup, decision models only; the PWS-Decisions rows of the charts above are the same means for Perplexity's two setups. Chat models, with and without their own web search, and every number: PWS-Decisions.

What the search returned

In short. On the full bank, 406 to 414 questions per grounded run had 8+ consecutive question words in a returned title or snippet, and 123 to 125 got the source book itself. A word match alone does not prove that the answer was found.

Unit: returned pages, up to 5 per question; each site's count is out of the run's total pages.

The 8+ word check. A literal-match check: runs of 8 or more consecutive words of the question (its body and each option's text, without the option letters) in a returned page's title or snippet. It shows only such matches. Our translations are unpublished, but that does not prove the answer, or equivalent information, cannot be found on the web.

Read it with these caveats

  • Grounded searches send the questions to Perplexity's search. Each question with web went out as a web search.
  • The pilot came first. Our protocol's first step was 200 questions (68 reading, 132 grammar; runs 631 and 632). Its rule: a full grounded run only for a gain of more than 5 points. The pilot gained +0.5 points, short of the rule; the full-bank grounded run was made afterwards so that the setup could be ranked.
  • Costs with web are an upper bound. ≤ Upper-bound estimate: one $0.001 web search counted per request; Perplexity bills only completed searches. Price: $0.02 per 1M input tokens plus $0.001 per completed search (pricing, read 5 November 2026). Search evidence counts as input tokens.
  • Runs per mode: 2 runs on the full bank and 1 run on the English questions. The cards, charts and first table show the mean of each mode's runs, with the lowest and highest run where there are several; Each run gives every run and its paired test. The ranking keeps each setup's first run. The pilot and the “Bilmiyorum” and “I don't know” pairs on our questions: one run each.
  • Perplexity's own figures. Perplexity reported 86 of 100 PWS-Decisions questions right with grounding (its run of 4 November 2026); the 51% without is the benchmark authors' (Parallel's) leaderboard figure, which Perplexity's own run matched. Its grounded requests took 370 ms at the median and 685 ms at the 95th percentile, from Perplexity's client (Web grounding for the Decisions API, 4 November 2026). Ours are from our machine, the mean of 3 runs per mode on 5 November 2026 to 6 November 2026: 50.7% off, 87.0% with web; 483 and 728 ms at the median and 95th percentile with web.
  • PWS-Decisions is gated. PWS-Decisions (version 0.1.0-rc1) is Parallel's dataset, for evaluation only; we publish aggregates only.

Our method

Model pplx-decider-v1.2 through Perplexity's Decisions API, one question per request, the Text tab's System One prompts. Each pair of runs differs only in the request's mode: off, or web_grounding, which runs a web search per question and adds its results as evidence. Runs from 5 November 2026 to 5 November 2026. Runs per mode: 2 runs on the full bank and 1 run on the English questions: each mode's first run and its repeats with the same questions in the same order, answer key, runner and settings; the ranking keeps each setup's first run, and the repeats enter only this page.

  • Turkish, full bank: 2,198 questions = 656 reading + 1,542 grammar (first runs 633 and 640, the Text tab rows Perplexity Decider 27B v1.2 (API) and Perplexity Decider 27B v1.2 (API, with web)). Repeats with the same questions in the same order: repeat 2: runs 684 and 685. Reading: units 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6.
  • Turkish pilot: 200 = 68 reading + 132 grammar, the runner's sample with seed 0 (runs 631 and 632).
  • “Bilmiyorum” as an extra option, as on the Hallucination tab: the full bank (2,198 questions, runs 641 and 642) and the pilot (200, runs 634 and 635). “I don't know” as an extra option in English, as on English and Turkish: the 547 kept questions (runs 643 and 644).
  • English: the 547 kept reading questions of the English pages in our translations (first runs 637 and 639).
  • PWS-Decisions: its 100 public English questions with the benchmark's own request, 3 runs per mode by us on 5 November 2026, as on PWS-Decisions; the source checks come from our first grounded run of 5 November 2026.

Accuracy: right answers over the questions asked. Time: each request's wall-clock time on our machine; the median and the 95th percentile by nearest rank. Cost per 1M questions: the run's input tokens at $0.02 per 1M plus $0.001 per request with web, over its questions. A mean of several runs: the mean of the runs' values, with the lowest and highest run. The paired test: the exact McNemar test on the questions one arm got right and the other did not, per pair of runs.

Download: results.json (every number on this page but PWS-Decisions' means, under web_grounding), answers.json (the pilots and the repeats under web_grounding_runs, the other runs in their own lists), web-grounding.json (tokens by group, the source checks' question ids and the first PWS-Decisions run's aggregates) and pws-decisions.json (PWS-Decisions' runs). reproduce.py recomputes results.json.
