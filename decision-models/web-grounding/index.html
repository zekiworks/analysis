Experiment

Web grounding: Perplexity Decider v1.2 off and with web

Perplexity's Decisions API can search the web for each question and add what it finds as evidence. Both setups are in the ranking: Perplexity Decider 27B v1.2 (API) and Perplexity Decider 27B v1.2 (with web). This page compares them on the same questions.

With web, accuracy went from 66.3% to 68.7% on our full Turkish bank of 2,198 questions (94 right only off, 147 only with web; exact McNemar p < 0.001, a difference beyond chance), from 71.0% to 71.5% on the 200-question pilot (8 right only off, 9 only with web; exact McNemar p = 1.000, within chance), from 88.5% to 90.5% on our 547 English questions (2 right only off, 13 only with web; exact McNemar p = 0.007, a difference beyond chance) and from 51.0% to 87.0% on PWS-Decisions (8 right only off, 44 only with web; exact McNemar p < 0.001, a difference beyond chance). On the full Turkish bank the median request took 1.9 times as long, and the cost per 1M questions went from $7.70 to ≤ $1,034.

What the search returned

Unit: returned pages, up to 5 per question; each site's count is out of the run's total pages.

The 8+ word check. A literal-match check: runs of 8 or more consecutive words of the question (its body and each option's text, without the option letters) in a returned page's title or snippet. It shows only such matches. Our translations are unpublished, but that does not prove the answer, or equivalent information, cannot be found on the web.

Read it with these caveats

  • Grounded searches send the questions to Perplexity's search. Each question with web went out as a web search.
  • The pilot came first. Our protocol's first step was 200 questions (68 reading, 132 grammar; runs 631 and 632). A full grounded run was due only for a gain of more than 5 points; the pilot gained +0.5 points. The full grounded run was made anyway, because Ümit asked for the setup in the ranking.
  • Costs with web are an upper bound. ≤ Upper-bound estimate: one $0.001 web search counted per request; Perplexity bills only completed searches. Price: $0.02 per 1M input tokens plus $0.001 per completed search (pricing, read 5 November 2026). Search evidence counts as input tokens.
  • One run per arm. The paired tests compare the two arms on the same questions.
  • Perplexity's own figures. Perplexity reported 86 of 100 PWS-Decisions questions right with grounding (its run of 4 November 2026); the 51% without is the benchmark authors' (Parallel's) leaderboard figure, which Perplexity's own run matched. Its grounded requests took 370 ms at the median and 685 ms at the 95th percentile, from Perplexity's client (Web grounding for the Decisions API, 4 November 2026). Ours are from our machine, run on 5 November 2026: 51% off, 87% with web; 479 and 631 ms at the median and 95th percentile with web.
  • PWS-Decisions is gated. PWS-Decisions (version 0.1.0-rc1) is Parallel's dataset, for evaluation only; we publish aggregates only.

Our method

Model pplx-decider-v1.2 through Perplexity's Decisions API, one question per request, the Text tab's System One prompts. Each pair of runs differs only in the request's mode: off, or web_grounding, which runs a web search per question and adds its results as evidence. Runs from 5 November 2026 to 5 November 2026.

  • Turkish, full bank: 2,198 questions = 656 reading + 1,542 grammar (runs 633 and 640, the Text tab rows Perplexity Decider 27B v1.2 (API) and Perplexity Decider 27B v1.2 (with web)). Reading: units 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6.
  • Turkish pilot: 200 = 68 reading + 132 grammar, the runner's sample with seed 0 (runs 631 and 632).
  • “Bilmiyorum” as a sixth option, as on the Hallucination tab: the full bank (2,198 questions, runs 641 and 642) and the pilot (200, runs 634 and 635). “I don't know” as a sixth option in English, as on English and Turkish: the 547 kept questions (runs 643 and 644).
  • English: the 547 kept reading questions of the English pages in our translations (runs 637 and 639).
  • PWS-Decisions: its 100 public English questions with the benchmark's own request, run by us on 5 November 2026.

Accuracy: right answers over the questions asked. Time: each request's wall-clock time on our machine; the median and the 95th percentile by nearest rank. Cost per 1M questions: the run's input tokens at $0.02 per 1M plus $0.001 per request with web, over its questions. The paired test: the exact McNemar test on the questions one arm got right and the other did not.

Download: results.json (every number on this page, under web_grounding), answers.json (the pilots under web_grounding_runs, the other runs in their own lists) and web-grounding.json (tokens by group, the source checks' question ids and PWS-Decisions' aggregates). reproduce.py recomputes them.
