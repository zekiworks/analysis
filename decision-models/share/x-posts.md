When should you trust an AI model to make a decision? When its confident answers turn out to be right.

We tested 18 decision models on a reading and grammar test in native Turkish (not translated): 2,198 questions. We put 15 of them in front of three LLMs. When the decision model was confident, we kept its answer; when it was not, the LLM answered.

Perplexity's Decider v1.1 took the most work off each LLM. Replaying our recorded answers:
Claude Sonnet 5.5: 48% cheaper, accuracy 0.10 points lower
Claude Opus 5.5: 25% cheaper, accuracy 0.23 points lower
GPT-6 Astra: 21% cheaper, accuracy 0.13 points lower

In a live test with new calls to each LLM, the estimated cost fell by less: 37% for Sonnet, 19% for Opus and 12% for Astra.

The test covers reading and grammar, not financial decisions. Test on your own task first.
