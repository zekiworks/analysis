How it's scored

  • Every bench on one scale: 0 = random guessing, 100 = perfect; higher is better ↑.
  • Reading (2,198 Turkish questions, 5 options) and Logic & Math: accuracy corrected for chance, (accuracy − 1/k) / (1 − 1/k) × 100, k the number of options. Vision: the same on Reading's 763 questions that depend on the printed page, read from the page image alone.
  • Backgammon: 0 = the error rate of a player that picks a random legal move, 100 = no error.
  • Combined = the plain mean of Reading (Text), Logic & Math and Backgammon, equal weights, only for a model that ran all three, one model on every bench (the rule); the others are in each bench's tab.
  • Learning from experience ("did it improve?", points gained, higher is better ↑) is a column of its own, outside the combined score.
  • Lower is better ↓ for calibration error (ECE, in points: how far the stated probabilities are from the hit rate) and latency. Close scores read tied, never with an interval: the rule.
