A late entry joins an original league table after it was played and plays every setup of that table once, in matches of its own. Its games count in no standings table, no league table, no Hype Cup input and no other setup's numbers. The numbers here come from its own late-entry games only, computed as for a setup in a league.

Error rate and risk judgement: 95% intervals, whole games resampled. Ability d′ and caution c at the 0.001 margin: rates corrected by adding 0.5 to the counts and 1 to the totals; 95% intervals, blocks of two seat-swapped games resampled. Cut off: answers that stopped at an output limit the request sent (finish reason length, stop reason max_tokens, finishReason MAX_TOKENS, incomplete max_output_tokens), in the stored response or error. Effort: the reasoning effort the requests sent. Engine's best play: the share of its scored decisions that chose the engine's best play (equity loss under the best-play threshold). Time: median request seconds per decision (waits between retries not included). Cost per 1,000 decisions: the recorded tokens at the price shown, as labelled there (list or billed price; for a setup on our GPUs the hosted model's list price, not billed).

Notes
Google's Gemini Decision 1 through OpenRouter, pinned to Google AI Studio (fallbacks off) for every decision; never on our GPUs. Late entry to the original League 1 table (not in standings, not eligible for the Hype Cup); its matches were played on 11 Nov 2026. It played 17 of its 19 opponents: its pair with Mercury Decide was not played (Mercury Decide is no longer served on OpenRouter; match #1016 aborted; outside the requested scope, Ümit, 12 Nov) and its pair with GPT-6 Luna was not played (GPT-6 Luna's API was out of credit; outside the requested scope, Ümit, 12 Nov). Two runner builds: dab6da5 for its first 9 matches (#993–#1013, before 21:46:31 UTC), with no probability-map check, and 38024d6 (map-check v2) from 21:46:31 UTC for its last 8 (#1053–#1063). Under map-check v2 a returned probability map is used only if it covers exactly the options shown, every value is finite and in [0, 1], and the values sum to 1 within 1e-6; this sum rule is new (no frozen sum tolerance existed), was chosen on 11 Nov 2026 and applies to this entry only; a failed map still plays the returned choice and stores no probabilities. An offline audit of the first matches under the same rule found no failing map. Option order: we send the options in our seeded order, as for every setup, while Google's docs say the API sorts the option keys alphabetically (UTF-8 byte order; an exact tie goes to the alphabetically first key), so the order the model sees is probably not ours (Google's docs; not verified by us). Its cost is the billed cost of each decision; the entry, with its paid opponents, billed $1.1819 in all.
