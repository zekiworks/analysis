A late entry joins an original league table after it was played and plays every setup of that table once, in matches of its own. Its games count in no standings table, no league table, no Hype Cup input and no other setup's numbers. The numbers here come from its own late-entry games only, computed as for a setup in a league.

Error rate and risk judgement: 95% intervals, whole games resampled. Ability d′ and caution c at the 0.001 margin: rates corrected by adding 0.5 to the counts and 1 to the totals; 95% intervals, blocks of two seat-swapped games resampled. Cut off: answers that stopped at an output limit the request sent (finish reason length, stop reason max_tokens, finishReason MAX_TOKENS, incomplete max_output_tokens), in the stored response or error. Effort: the reasoning effort the requests sent. Engine's best play: the share of its scored decisions that chose the engine's best play (equity loss under the best-play threshold). Time: median request seconds per decision (waits between retries not included). Cost per 1,000 decisions: the recorded tokens at the price shown, as labelled there (list or billed price; for a setup on our GPUs the hosted model's list price, not billed).

Notes
Late entry to the original League 1 table (not in standings, not eligible for the Hype Cup), late-entry league #63 (Can #11963, 18 Nov 2026). SubQ's Savant 1.4 through SubQ's OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions API, never on our GPUs; the League 1 letter prompt with the letter constrained to the options (strict json_schema enum), as for Muse Embermax. SubQ refuses logprobs for this model (400 unsupported_parameter), so it answers with one letter, read from the reply, no probability; 7.87 M of its 7.89 M output tokens were reasoning. First stage 18 Nov 2026, 06:41–08:12 UTC: its program and hosted opponents (#1558–#1566); local stage 19 Nov 2026, 07:18–09:20 UTC: the 8 setups we run ourselves, one at a time on our GPU 0 (#1567–#1574). Runner 38024d6 for every match, started by the scripts on branch late-savant (1198670, b15609f). It played 16 of its 19 opponents: its pairs with GPT-6 Luna and Mercury Decide were not played (outside the requested scope), and its pair with GLiDEAPI (Fastino's hosted API) was not played because that API answers 402, out of funds, since 18 Nov ~08:00 UTC and no funds could be added (Can #12559; match #1565 aborted before any move); not a loss. GLiDEv1.2 · NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, the released weights on our server, was played. Invalid answers: 0. Entry spend $4.35 (it and its paid opponents).
