A late entry joins an original league table after it was played and plays every setup of that table once, in matches of its own. Its games count in no standings table, no league table, no Hype Cup input and no other setup's numbers. The numbers here come from its own late-entry games only, computed as for a setup in a league.

Error rate and risk judgement: 95% intervals, whole games resampled. Ability d′ and caution c at the 0.001 margin: rates corrected by adding 0.5 to the counts and 1 to the totals; 95% intervals, blocks of two seat-swapped games resampled. Cut off: answers that stopped at an output limit the request sent (finish reason length, stop reason max_tokens, finishReason MAX_TOKENS, incomplete max_output_tokens), in the stored response or error. Effort: the reasoning effort the requests sent. Engine's best play: the share of its scored decisions that chose the engine's best play (equity loss under the best-play threshold). Time: median request seconds per decision (waits between retries not included). Cost per 1,000 decisions: the recorded tokens at the price shown, as labelled there (list or billed price; for a setup on our GPUs the hosted model's list price, not billed).

Late entry to the original League 1 table (not in standings, not eligible for the Hype Cup), late-entry league #41. fastino/GLiDE-2B @ 00f122c on our DGX Spark (spark2, the whole GB10, handed over by logic one server at a time), BF16, through the server its repository ships (python -m glide.server, GLIDE_MAX_BATCH=1), one request at a time; never on a hosted API. First stage 12 Nov 2026: its 9 hosted and program opponents (#1191–#1199, runner e4ce0e9); local stage 13 Nov, 14 Nov 2026: the 8 setups we run ourselves, one at a time on an RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell of ours (#1298–#1305, runner 2a22d1f). It played 17 of its 19 opponents: its pair with Mercury Decide was not played (Mercury Decide is no longer served on OpenRouter; match #1200 aborted at its warm-up; outside the requested scope, Ümit, 12 Nov) and its pair with GPT-6 Luna was not played (GPT-6 Luna's API was out of credit; outside the requested scope, Ümit, 12 Nov). It never truncates: a prompt over GLIDE_MAX_LEN 40,000 tokens is refused (422); League 1's longest stored request (251 options) is 28,323 tokens; nothing refused. Its cost is an estimate from its time on the DGX Spark (GPU time, at most).
