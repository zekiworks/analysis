Learning: did they improve?

The leaderboard's Learning tab shows, for every model, its verdict where one is published, else its state in the learning lanes: running, waiting (and why), not tested (and the rule), or not in the learning plan.

How a model is judged. It answers the 200-position risk test 3 times with no memory (before = the mean of those empty runs), then plays 5 series of 10 games against GNUbg weak, writing its own lessons after each game, and takes the test again after game 10 of each series (after = the mean of the 5). Improved? is Yes when risks found rises by more than the improvement threshold (the spread, max − min, of its own 3 empty runs, at least 5 points) and right safety falls by no more than 5 points. This is an operational threshold, not a statistical test. Yes or No only once every run is complete.

How the scores work

  1. Models play full games of backgammon. GNU Backgammon scores every move.
  2. Risk judgement: did the model find a worthwhile risk when risk paid, and play safe when safe was right? 50 = always plays safe, 100 = every call right.
  3. Error rate: how much a move gives away, judged by the engine (lower is better).
  4. Everything is public: every position, every legal play, the engine's value for each.
  5. Leagues played different positions, so close scores across leagues are not a clear ranking.
