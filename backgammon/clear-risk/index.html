The scan: which decisions have a clear risk call

Preliminary / partial

  • A decision is scanned when the mover has both a safe play (0 hitting rolls for the opponent) and an unsafe play (12 or more hitting rolls), there is contact, and there are more than 2 legal plays. Invalid answers (fallback) are skipped.
  • Gap = 2-ply cubeless equity of the best unsafe play minus that of the best safe play (GNU Backgammon). Risk wins: gap of +0.04 or more. Safety wins: gap of -0.04 or less. Both are 'flagged'.
  • 3-ply stability check (it replaced rollouts for the leagues): a flagged decision is kept only if the gap is 0.04 or more at both 2-ply and 3-ply, has the same sign at both, and the two gaps differ by 0.02 or less. It is a stability signal, not a rollout.
  • Kept: 25,199 of 32,840 flagged decisions (7,997 Risk wins, 17,202 Safety wins).
  • The method was written down and committed before any computation; the 3-ply rule was committed before any 3-ply value was computed.

Checks on the scan

Preliminary / partial

  • The best legal play is in between (1 to 11 hitting rolls) in 22% of Risk wins and 16% of Safety wins: in about one decision in five or six, neither of the two plays the scan compares is the best play.
  • Bands are the absolute 3-ply gap: 0.04-0.08, 0.08-0.15 and 0.15+ (lower edge included). Setups (pooled) = the 45 setups other than the three programs, Random, Safest and GNUbg weak; each decision counts once.
  • Missed risks do not fall as the gap grows (59%, 65%, 55%); needless risks do (6.0%, 4.7%, 2.4%).
  • Equity lost = 2-ply equity of the best legal play minus that of the chosen play, mean (count). Took the right risk: a Risk win played unsafe, within 0.02 of the best unsafe play.
  • Not paired: the five Part-2 models played in different leagues.
